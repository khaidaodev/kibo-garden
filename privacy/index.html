Kibo Garden

Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

The short version

Kibo Garden does not collect any data. Your data stays on your device. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

What is stored, and where

Your tasks, projects, labels, habits, check-ins, notes, garden and settings are saved in a database inside the app on your device. Nobody else can see them, including me.

Garden location and seasons

The garden can follow the sun and the seasons of a city you choose from a list built into the app, or of your device's time zone. The app never asks for your location and never looks anything up online.

Pictures of your island

Share my island makes a picture of your island on your device, with its name and, if you choose, your streak. No tasks or habits are in it. It only leaves your device if you share it, and you choose where it goes.

Sounds

The ambient sounds and tick sounds are part of the app. Nothing is downloaded or streamed.

Automatic backups

Once a day the app saves a backup of your data on your device, in its own folder, keeping the last seven. These backups stay on your device and are deleted with the app. You can turn them off in Settings.

Backups, transfers and exports

Your data only leaves your device when you choose to share it. If you save a backup copy, a CSV file, or a transfer file to move your data to another device, the app hands the file to the share sheet. You choose where it goes, for example Files, iCloud Drive or an email. After that, the file is looked after by whatever you picked, under that service's own privacy terms. The app never sends anything anywhere by itself.

Asking for help

If you tap Need help?, your mail app opens a new email to me. You write it and you send it. It includes only the app version, the kind of device (iPhone, iPad or Mac) and its system version, and, if the app hit a problem, one short line about the error with nothing from your tasks or habits in it. Nothing is sent unless you send it, and you can delete those lines first.

Sync between devices

This version does not sync. Optional iCloud sync may come in a future update. If it does, it will be optional, and this policy will be updated first.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled by the app on your device. They are not sent through any server.

Third parties

The app has no third party services that collect or send data. No advertising, analytics or crash reporting code is included.

Deleting your data

You can delete everything at any time in Settings, under Delete all data. The automatic backups from before stay on your device, in case you change your mind, until newer ones replace them or you delete the app. Deleting the app deletes all of its data, including its automatic backups. Backup copies you saved somewhere else, for example in Files, stay there until you delete them.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Who makes Kibo Garden

Kibo Garden is made by Khai Dao, an independent developer in the United Kingdom, who is responsible for this policy. Because the app collects nothing, I hold no information about you, apart from any email you choose to send me.

Emails you send me

If you email me, I use your email only to reply and help. I never share it, never add you to a mailing list and never send you marketing emails. I delete support emails once they are no longer needed, and straight away if you ask.

Your rights

Wherever you live, including the UK, the EU and California, you can ask me what I hold about you, and ask me to correct it or delete it. For almost everyone the answer is nothing, because the app collects nothing. I do not sell or share personal information, and never have. If you are in the UK or the EU and unhappy with how I handled a request, you can complain to your data protection authority, such as the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) in the UK.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be in the app and on this page, with a new date.

This website

This website has no cookies, analytics or tracking of its own, and loads nothing from other websites. It is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may keep short technical logs, such as IP addresses, to run and protect the service, under GitHub's privacy statement. I never see them.
