Kibo Garden

Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

The short version

Kibo Garden does not collect any data. Everything you type into it stays on your own devices, and in your own private iCloud if you sync. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

What is stored, and where

Your tasks, projects, labels, habits, check-ins, notes and settings are saved in a database inside the app on your device. Nobody else can see them, including me.

iCloud sync (optional)

With iCloud sync on, your data is also copied to your own private iCloud, so your iPhone, iPad and Mac signed in to the same Apple Account show the same tasks, habits and garden. It is kept in a private area of iCloud that only this app on your own devices can read. It goes from your devices to Apple's iCloud and back. It never goes to me, and I can never see it. iCloud is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. You choose whether to sync when you first open the app, and you can turn it off any time in Settings.

Backups and exports

If you export a backup, a CSV file, or a transfer file for your other devices, the app hands the file to the share sheet. You choose where it goes, for example Files, iCloud Drive or an email. After that, the file is looked after by whatever you picked, under that service's own privacy terms. The app never sends it anywhere by itself.

Asking for help

If you tap Need help?, your mail app opens a new email to me with the app version, the kind of device (iPhone, iPad or Mac) and its system version filled in. Nothing is sent unless you send it, and you can delete those lines first.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled by the app on your device. They are not sent through any server.

Third parties

The app has no third party services that collect or send data. No advertising, analytics or crash reporting code is included.

Deleting your data

You can delete everything at any time in Settings, under Delete all data. With iCloud sync on, choose "All my devices" to remove it from your other devices and your iCloud too. Deleting the app deletes its data on that device. You can also remove the app's iCloud data in the Settings app, under your name, then iCloud.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be in the app and on this page, with a new date.

This website

This website has no cookies, analytics or tracking of its own, and loads nothing from other websites. It is hosted by GitHub Pages.
