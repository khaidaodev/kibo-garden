Kibo Garden

Support

Kibo Garden is a to-do list and habit tracker for iPhone, iPad and Mac where every habit grows a plant in your garden.

Contact

Email khai.dao.dev@gmail.com with any question, problem or idea. I usually reply within a few days.

When reporting a problem, it helps to include which device you use (iPhone, iPad or Mac), its software version, and what you tapped just before it went wrong.

Questions

Settings is in the sidebar on iPad and Mac. On iPhone, tap Browse, then Settings.

Can I use a keyboard on iPad and Mac?

Yes. Cmd+N adds a task, Cmd+F searches, Esc closes a sheet, and Cmd+1 to Cmd+5 jump between the main sections. On iPad, hold Cmd to see them all. They're also listed in Settings.

How do I back up my data?

Open Settings, then Export backup (JSON). The share sheet opens, so you can save the file to Files, iCloud Drive or anywhere else you like. To bring it back, use Import backup in the same place. You can merge it with what is already there, or replace everything.

It is a good idea to save a backup now and then, even with iCloud sync on.

Does Kibo Garden sync between my iPhone, iPad and Mac?

Yes, through your own private iCloud. Turn on Sync with iCloud when you first open the app, or later in Settings. Use the same Apple Account on every device, with iCloud Drive turned on. Your data goes from your devices to your iCloud and back. I can never see it.

If sync says you're not signed in to iCloud, open the Settings app (System Settings on a Mac), tap your name, then iCloud, and make sure iCloud Drive is on. Changes can take a minute to reach your other devices. Sync now in Settings checks straight away.

Why do reminders only come on my iPhone?

So you don't get the same reminder on every device. Send reminders on this device is on for iPhone and off for iPad and Mac to start with. Change it in Settings, under Reminders, on each device.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings, then Delete all data, and confirm twice. This removes every task, habit, check-in and setting and cannot be undone. With iCloud sync on, it asks whether to delete on This device only or on All my devices (which also clears your iCloud copy). Deleting the app removes its data from that device only.

Do you collect any of my data?

No. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no ads. Read the full privacy policy.

Why is my plant wilting?

Plants droop when a habit misses its scheduled days several times in a row, and recover when you tick it again. A plant that has died can be revived with plant food, which you earn by ticking habits and finishing tasks.

Reminders don't appear

Check that notifications are allowed for Kibo Garden in the Settings app, under Notifications, and that Send reminders on this device is on in Kibo Garden's Settings. Then open the habit or task and make sure a reminder time is set.
