Kibo Garden

Support

Kibo Garden is a to-do list and habit tracker for iPhone, iPad and Mac where every habit grows a plant on your own little island garden.

Contact

Email khai.dao.dev@gmail.com with any question, problem or idea. I usually reply within a few days.

The easiest way is Need help? in Settings: it opens an email to me with the app version, your kind of device and its system version already filled in, so you only need to write what happened. You write and send it yourself, and you can delete those lines first.

Questions

Settings is in the sidebar on iPad and Mac. On iPhone, tap Browse, then Settings.

How do backups work?

Kibo Garden saves a backup on your device once a day, keeping the last seven. You'll find them in Settings, under Backups, where each one can be restored or shared. These automatic backups stay on your device and are deleted if you delete the app, so now and then tap Save a copy to Files and keep the copy somewhere safe, like iCloud Drive.

To bring a backup back, tap Restore next to it, or Import backup for a file you saved. You can merge it with what is already there, or replace everything. A backup of the device is always saved first, just in case.

How do I move my data to another device?

On the device that has your data, open Settings, then Send to my other devices, and save the file to iCloud Drive. On the other device, open Settings, then Get from my other devices, and pick that file. You'll see what it adds before anything changes, and a backup of that device is saved first. Ticks, Kira and purchases are never counted twice, so you can move data back and forth as often as you like.

Does Kibo Garden sync on its own?

Not in this version. Use Send to my other devices and Get from my other devices instead. Optional iCloud sync may come in a future update.

What is Kira?

Kira is a sparkly reward you earn by ticking habits and finishing tasks. You spend it in the island shop on decorations, land and plant food. It is only ever earned, never bought: there is nothing to pay for in Kibo Garden.

How does the island work?

Every habit and repeating task grows a plant on your island, from a seed to a full bush. A little droplet shows over plants that are due today. Open the Garden tab, then the round leaf button for Arrange, Focus, Shop, Inventory, Share my island and Island settings (the island's name, its day or night, Classic or 3D, the garden location and Seasons). In Arrange, hold and drag things to move them, and tap a wooden price tag to buy more land. Prefer a list? Tap List at the top.

Can I have more than one island?

Yes. More islands are in the shop, each with its own look. Swipe across the island's name, or tap the arrows beside it, to go between them. Plants can be moved from one island to another.

What is the 3D garden?

In Island settings, Garden style switches between Classic and 3D on that device. Drag to move around, pinch to zoom and tap the round arrow to turn the island. If 3D can't run smoothly on a device, it goes back to Classic by itself and says so quietly. With VoiceOver on, the List is the easiest way to look after your plants.

What do Seasons do?

Turn on Seasons in Island settings and your islands follow the real season where your garden is: blossom in spring, full green in summer, warm leaves in autumn and a little snow in winter, the right way round south of the equator. Near the equator it stays green all year. Choose where your garden is with Garden location: a city from a list in the app, or your device's time zone. The app never asks for your location.

How do I share a picture of my island?

Tap the round leaf button, then Share my island. You'll see the picture first: your island as it looks right now, with its name and, if you like, your streak. No tasks or habits are in it. Tap Share to send or save it.

What is the Top 3?

Star up to three tasks or habits that matter most today, and they wait at the top of Today until they're done. Finish all three for a little Kira. Star something by swiping a task, from its menu, or on its own screen. The Top 3 only shows on Today while something is starred.

What are the evening wrap-up and Your week?

In the evening, a small card on Today lets you move what's left to tomorrow in one go. Turn it off, or have it send a gentle reminder, in Settings, under Today. Your week, a calm look back at the week before, is in Stats.

How does the focus timer work?

Open a task or habit and tap the hourglass, or tap Focus in the island menu. Choose how long, and a soft sunbeam falls on that plant while you focus. Finishing a session gives a little Kira, and the minutes show in that task's or habit's stats.

Can a habit have steps?

Yes. When editing a yes or no habit, add Steps, for example Water, Stretch and Journal for a morning routine. Tick them one by one on Today, and the habit is done when every step is.

What are saved filters?

In Browse, under Filters, make a list of tasks by project, label, priority or date, for example everything urgent this week. You can also type a short query like p1 & this week or #Work | @phone.

What is Zen view?

Tap the leaf at the top of Today to see only the next three things, and tap it again to see everything.

What is a rest token?

A rest token keeps a habit's streak and its plant over one missed day, as a rest day. Buy them with Kira in the shop, under Plant care (you can keep two), then use one from that day in the habit's history.

Can I play calm sounds?

Yes. In Settings, under Sounds, choose an ambient sound (soft rain, birds, wind, a stream or night crickets), its volume, and whether it plays while you focus, on the island, or both. It is off until you choose one.

Can I add a task from the Shortcuts app?

Yes. In Shortcuts, add Ask for Input, then Open URL with kibogarden://quick-add?text= followed by the input. Name it, for example, "Add a task to Kibo Garden". The task opens in Kibo Garden for you to check before adding, and dates like "tomorrow 9am" or "p1" work as usual.

Why is my plant wilting?

Plants droop when a habit misses its scheduled days several times in a row, and recover when you tick it again. A plant that has died can be revived with Kira.

Can I choose a different plant?

Yes. Open the habit, tap Edit, and pick one under Choose your plant. It keeps its growth and health; only the look changes.

Can I hide the sidebar on iPad and Mac?

Yes. Tap the sidebar button at the top left, or press Control+Cmd+S, and the page gets the whole width. Tap it again, or swipe in from the left edge, to bring the sidebar back. Kibo Garden remembers your choice on each device.

Can I use a keyboard on iPad and Mac?

Yes:

  • Cmd+N adds a task, Shift+Cmd+N adds a habit and Shift+Cmd+P adds a project
  • Cmd+F searches, and Esc closes a sheet
  • Control+Cmd+S hides or shows the sidebar
  • Cmd+1 to Cmd+5 go to Today, Upcoming, Garden, Stats and Inbox
  • Left and Right arrows move the week or month in stats and calendars

On iPad, hold Cmd to see them all. They're also listed in Settings. On the island, two fingers on a trackpad move it and a pinch zooms.

Why do reminders only come on my iPhone?

So you don't get the same reminder on every device. Send reminders on this device is on for iPhone and off for iPad and Mac to start with. Change it in Settings, under Reminders, on each device.

Reminders don't appear

Check that notifications are allowed for Kibo Garden in the Settings app, under Notifications, and that Send reminders on this device is on in Kibo Garden's Settings. Then open the habit or task and make sure a reminder time is set.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings, then Delete all data, and confirm twice. This removes every task, habit, check-in, island and setting from that device, so save a backup copy first if you might want it. The automatic backups from before stay in Backups for a while, in case you change your mind, until newer ones replace them. Deleting the app deletes all of its data, including its automatic backups. Copies you saved in Files stay there until you delete them.

Do you collect any of my data?

No. Your data stays on your device. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Read the full privacy policy.

Something went wrong

If the app ever shows "Something went wrong. Your data is safe.", tap Try again. If it happens again, tap Export backup to keep a copy, then Need help? to tell me. That email also includes a short line about the error, with nothing from your tasks or habits in it.
